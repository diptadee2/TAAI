GATE DA 2027 Syllabus: Complete Breakdown

The GATE Data Science & AI (DA) paper covers a wide spread of topics — probability and statistics, linear algebra, calculus, machine learning, and core CS fundamentals. Here's how to think about prioritizing your prep.

Section-wise breakdown

  • Probability & Statistics — consistently one of the highest-weighted sections.
  • Linear Algebra — matrix operations, eigenvalues, and vector spaces show up across both the math and ML sections.
  • Machine Learning — supervised/unsupervised learning, model evaluation.
  • Programming & Data Structures — Python-based questions on core CS concepts.

How to prioritize

  1. Start with probability and statistics — it has the highest weight and underpins the ML section too.
  2. Build linear algebra fundamentals before moving into ML-heavy topics.
  3. Practice with GATE-style questions early, not just textbook problems.

We'll keep this post updated as the official syllabus is confirmed each year.
