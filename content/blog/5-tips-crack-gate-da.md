Six months is enough time to crack GATE DA if you prepare with focus. Here's a plan that works.

## 1. Start with the math, not the ML

Probability, statistics, and linear algebra carry the most weight — and they're the foundation for the ML section too. Get these solid first.

## 2. Practice GATE-style questions early

Textbook problems and GATE questions are different. Start solving previous years' papers from month one, not month five.

## 3. Track weak topics weekly

Don't just take tests — review *why* you got a question wrong. A topic-wise weakness log is more useful than a score.

## 4. Build a revision cycle

Plan for at least 2 full revision passes before the exam. New content should stop a month before GATE.

## 5. Simulate exam conditions

Full-length mock tests, timed, with no breaks — at least 6-8 of them in the last two months.

Consistency over intensity. A focused 3-4 hours a day for 6 months beats sporadic 10-hour cram sessions.
